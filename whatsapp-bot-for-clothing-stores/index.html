A WhatsApp sales bot for clothing stores.

“Is this available in medium?”, “Do you have it in black?”, “Price?” — fashion customers ask before every order, often late at night or the moment you post a new collection. Webxeen answers on your shop’s WhatsApp in the customer’s own language, books the order with a receipt and sends it straight to you.

In short: Webxeen is an AI assistant for your clothing shop’s WhatsApp number. It answers only from your own product list and prices — including the sizes and colours you put in it — takes orders 24/7 in 8 languages, collects name, address and payment method, sends a receipt with an order number, checks payment screenshots and makes repeat cash-on-delivery returners pay in advance.

An example order

From “is it in my size?” to a booked order — without you typing.

The customer asks about size and colour. The assistant answers from your product list, quotes the price (it never invents one), adds your delivery fee and asks the customer to confirm. Then it collects the name, address and payment method and sends a receipt with an order number.

For a bank transfer, card or mobile-wallet payment it asks for a slightly odd amount — never more than the total — so you can match each payment in your bank app at a glance. When the customer sends the payment screenshot, it is checked and arrives on your phone with buttons.

If the customer doesn’t pay, you don’t have to chase them: unpaid online orders get a reminder after 24 hours and are cancelled automatically after 72 hours.

Cash on delivery

Fewer returned COD parcels.

In fashion, a refused cash-on-delivery parcel costs you twice: the delivery both ways, and stock that sat out of your shop for days. Webxeen keeps a record for every customer.

When a parcel comes back, type returned 18. After 2 returned COD parcels, that customer must pay in advance next time — the assistant handles it, so you don’t have the awkward conversation.

Customers can still cancel by themselves before an order is dispatched, which is better than a refused parcel at the door.

Your side

Run your shop from WhatsApp — no new app.

One tap per order

Every new order and payment screenshot arrives with buttons: Paid, Not paid, Dispatched, Cancel. The customer is told automatically.

Payment check

Screenshots are read and compared with the order, and reused or edited ones are flagged before you send anything.

Complaints with numbers

Wrong item, late parcel, damaged box: each complaint reaches your team as C1, C2… and you close it with solved C3.

A calm morning list

Quiet hours keep your phone silent from 11 PM to 8 AM. At 8 AM you get one list: payments to check, parcels to send, refunds due and open complaints.

Tap buttons on WhatsApp, payment-screenshot checking and complaints to your team are in the Gold and Premium plans; Silver sends order alerts by email with a morning summary. Compare plans.

FAQ

Clothing shop questions, answered.

How many products can I add?

Silver covers up to 50 products, Gold up to 300 and Premium is unlimited.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

Usually yes — a number that is not already on WhatsApp. Or send us your current number and we will check if it can be connected.

Most of my customers find me on Instagram. Does it work there?

WhatsApp is live now. Instagram DMs and Facebook Messenger are coming soon, with the same assistant, products and order flow. Auto replies to “Price?” comments by DM (comment-to-DM) are also coming soon.

Can it book my courier?

Courier and payment-link integrations are coming soon in the Premium plan. Today you mark orders dispatched with one tap or a short command, and the customer is told automatically.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It only answers from your own products and prices and stays on topic. You confirm every payment yourself in your own bank or mobile-wallet app, and complaints go straight to your team with numbers like C1.

Does it work outside Pakistan?

Yes. Each business has its own currency and time zone, and the assistant replies in 8 languages, so a clothing shop in the Gulf, the UK, Europe or anywhere else on WhatsApp can use it.
