An example order

A new drop goes live at 11 PM. What happens to the messages?

The assistant replies to every customer at once, from your own product list: the price, and the sizes and colours you have listed for that item. It never invents a price or a product you don’t sell.

When the customer decides, it adds up the items and shipping, asks them to confirm, collects the name, address and payment method and sends a receipt with an order number.

The order lands on your own WhatsApp with buttons. If an item sold out in the meantime, one line — cancel 18 out of stock — cancels it and the customer is told automatically.

Everyday messages

What fashion customers ask on WhatsApp — and what the assistant does.

Customer writesWhat Webxeen does
“Price?” / “How much is the blue kurta?”Answers from your own product list and prices only. It never invents a price or an item.
“Is this in medium?” / “Black available?”Answers from the sizes and colours you put in your product list.
“I want 2 of these”Collects items, sizes, quantities, name, address and payment method, confirms the total and sends a receipt with an order number.
“Can I pay online instead of COD?”The customer can switch payment method after booking, e.g. from cash on delivery to a bank transfer or a mobile wallet.

Please note: no AI can catch every fake payment. Always confirm the money in your own bank or mobile-wallet app before the parcel leaves.

Returns and cash on delivery

Fewer parcels that come back unpaid.

For clothing sellers, a returned cash-on-delivery parcel means two shipping costs and no sale. Webxeen keeps count for you: after a customer returns 2 COD parcels, they must pay in advance for their next order.

You record what happened with one short line on WhatsApp, and the customer is told automatically. Every action asks a quick Yes/No first, and you can undo it if you change your mind.

Short commands for busy days

Type one line instead of tapping each order:

FAQ

Clothing shop questions, answered.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

Usually yes — a number that is not already on WhatsApp. Or send us your current number and we will check if it can be connected.

How many products can I add?

Silver covers up to 50 products, Gold up to 300 and Premium is unlimited.

Can it reply to “price?” comments on Instagram?

Not yet. WhatsApp is live now. Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger and auto-replying to “Price?” comments by DM are coming soon.

We have a shop and an online store on different numbers.

The Premium plan includes up to 3 numbers or branches.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It only answers from your own products and prices and stays on topic. You confirm every payment yourself in your own bank or mobile-wallet app, and complaints go straight to your team with numbers like C1.

Does it work outside Pakistan?

Yes. Each business has its own currency and time zone, and the assistant replies in 8 languages, so a fashion shop in the Gulf, the UK, Europe or anywhere else on WhatsApp can use it.
