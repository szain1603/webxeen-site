How we keep it smooth

Motion that helps, never fights the visitor.

  • Your scroll stays yours. We never change the speed of the mouse wheel or finger swipe.
  • A calm first screen. The page opens clean and fast; the show starts when the visitor scrolls.
  • Smooth on phones. Light animation tools (GSAP and native CSS) and smaller 3D files, tested on slow phones.
  • Readable without motion. Visitors who turn off animations still see every word and picture.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and whether you need 3D. Message us with what you sell and we send a clear price before any work starts.

I don’t have a 3D model of my product. Is that a problem?

No. If you have one, we use it. If not, we can build a scroll story from your photos, or have a 3D model made for an extra cost.

Will it be slow on phones?

We test every page on low-cost phones. Heavy effects are switched off on weak devices so the page stays smooth.

Can it work with the Webxeen WhatsApp assistant?

Yes. Every page can end with an order button that opens WhatsApp, where the assistant answers and takes the order.
