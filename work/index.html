AI Receptionist for a Dental Clinic

Built for a sample clinic, "Areesha Dental Clinic", Model Town Link Road, Lahore. Patient names and numbers shown are test data.

The problem

A receptionist reads every appointment request by hand, copies names and numbers into a register, and has to judge which patient needs urgent attention. Requests that come in after hours wait until morning.

The solution

An online appointment form connected to an n8n workflow. Google Gemini reads each request, flags emergencies, checks clinic hours, and the details land in a Google Sheet with an email alert to the doctor within seconds, day or night.

How it works

  1. Patient fills the form with name, phone, preferred time and problem.
  2. Gemini AI reads the request and returns three decisions: is it urgent, is the clinic open at that time, and a clean date.
  3. Google Sheets gets a new row with the patient details and the AI's decisions.
  4. Gmail alerts the doctor. Urgent cases arrive with a 🚨 URGENT subject so they stand out in the inbox.

Booking Chatbot that Checks the Doctor's Schedule

Built for the same sample clinic. Bookings shown are test data.

The problem

A chatbot that only says "the clinic will confirm" still leaves staff to check the diary and call every patient back. Patients asking at night wait until the next day.

The solution

An n8n AI Agent with memory and two tools. It reads the clinic's booking register before offering a time, suggests the nearest free 15-minute slots, collects name, phone and problem one question at a time, then writes the booking itself and confirms.

How it works

  1. Patient asks for a time in chat, in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English. The bot works out the real date from words like "Thursday" or "kal".
  2. check_bookings reads the register in Google Sheets.
  3. If the slot is taken, the bot offers the nearest free 15-minute slots that day.
  4. book_appointment writes the booking to the sheet, and only then does the bot confirm.
