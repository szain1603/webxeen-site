An example order

What happens when a hungry customer messages you late at night?

The assistant replies straight away, quotes prices from your menu (it never invents a price), adds your delivery fee and asks the customer to confirm. Then it collects the name, address and payment method and sends a clean receipt with an order number.

If the customer changes their mind about payment after booking — say from cash on delivery to a bank transfer — they can switch. For online payments the assistant asks for a slightly odd amount, never more than the total, so you can spot each payment in your bank app at a glance.

You get the order on your own WhatsApp with buttons. Tap Dispatched when the rider leaves and the customer is told automatically.

Everyday messages

The messages restaurants get every day — and what the assistant does.

Customer writesWhat Webxeen does
“Menu?” / “How much is the biryani?”Answers from your own menu and price list only. It never invents prices or dishes.
“I want to order”Collects items, quantities, name, address and payment method, confirms the total and sends a receipt with an order number.
“Can I pay by card instead?”The customer can switch payment method after booking, e.g. from cash on delivery to bank transfer or a mobile wallet.
“Please cancel my order”Customers can cancel by themselves before the order is dispatched.
A payment screenshotReads the amount, transaction ID, date and receiver, and warns you if the amount is lower than due, the money went to a different account, the screenshot looks edited or was already used for another order.
“My food arrived cold”Logs a numbered complaint (C1, C2…) for your team and tells the customer they will get a reply within 24 hours.
“When did I last order?”Remembers returning customers and their past orders, and answers with the real order history.
Off-topic chatPolitely keeps the conversation about your restaurant.

Please note: no AI can catch every fake payment. Always confirm the money in your own bank or mobile-wallet app before the food leaves the kitchen.

Your side

Run the evening rush from WhatsApp — no new app.

One tap per order

Every new order and payment screenshot arrives with buttons: Paid, Not paid, Dispatched, Cancel. Each action asks a quick Yes/No first, then the customer is told automatically.

Short commands for busy nights

When several riders leave at once, type one line instead of tapping each order:

Fewer fake orders

After 2 returned cash-on-delivery orders, that customer must pay in advance. Payment screenshots are checked for you before the food goes out.

A calm morning list

Quiet hours keep your phone silent from 11 PM to 8 AM. At 8 AM you get one list: payments to check, orders to send, refunds due and open complaints.

Tap buttons on WhatsApp and payment-screenshot checking are in the Gold and Premium plans; Silver sends order alerts by email with a morning summary. Compare plans.

FAQ

Restaurant questions, answered.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

Usually yes — a number that is not already on WhatsApp. Or send us your current number and we will check if it can be connected.

How big can my menu be?

Silver covers up to 50 products, Gold up to 300 and Premium is unlimited.

We have more than one branch. Does that work?

The Premium plan includes up to 3 numbers or branches.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It only answers from your own menu and prices and stays on topic. You confirm every payment yourself in your own bank or mobile-wallet app, and complaints go straight to your team with numbers like C1.

Does it work on Instagram too?

WhatsApp is live now. Instagram DMs and Facebook Messenger are coming soon, with the same assistant, menu and order flow.

Does it work outside Pakistan?

Yes. Each business has its own currency and time zone, and the assistant replies in 8 languages, so a restaurant in the Gulf, the UK, Europe or anywhere else on WhatsApp can use it.
