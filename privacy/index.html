1. Who we are

Webxeen provides an AI sales assistant that answers customers on WhatsApp for small businesses. Webxeen is based in Model Town, Lahore, Pakistan. You can reach us at hello@webxeen.com.

2. Our role for sellers and their customers

When a customer messages a seller that uses Webxeen, the seller decides how its customer information is used. Webxeen handles that information on the seller’s behalf, only to run the seller’s assistant. If you are a customer of a seller, the seller’s own privacy notice also applies, and the seller is your first point of contact for questions about your orders and data.

For our own website visitors and for the sellers who sign up with us, Webxeen decides how the information is used.

3. Information we handle

Visitors to webxeen.com

  • Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not ask you to create an account.
  • Your browser stores your language choice and price-region choice (PKR or USD) on your own device so the site remembers them. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.
  • Our website host keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email (for example to ask for a demo), we receive your phone number or email address and your message.

Sellers (businesses using Webxeen)

  • Business details: business name, the WhatsApp number connected to Webxeen, currency and time zone.
  • Shop information you give us: products, prices, delivery rules and payment methods.
  • Contact details for alerts: the WhatsApp numbers and email addresses that receive order alerts, complaints and summaries.
  • Billing records: invoices, the amount and date of each payment, and the name on the paying account. Sellers in Pakistan pay by bank transfer; sellers abroad pay through Payoneer. We do not receive or store full card numbers.

Customers who message a seller

  • WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • Messages exchanged with the seller’s assistant.
  • Order details: products, quantity, name, delivery address, payment method and order number.
  • Payment screenshots sent in the chat, and the details read from them (amount, transaction ID, date and receiver).
  • Complaints, cancellations, refunds, returns and past orders, so the assistant can remember returning customers.

4. How we use it

  • To run each seller’s assistant: reply to customers, take and confirm orders, send receipts, check payment screenshots, send payment reminders, cancel online orders that stay unpaid, and alert the seller’s team.
  • To send sellers order alerts, morning summaries and reports.
  • To give support, set up accounts and bill for plans.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. We do not use customer chats or sellers’ data to train AI models.

5. Who we share it with

  • The seller the customer is talking to: they see their own customers’ orders, messages, payment screenshots and complaints.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): messages are sent and received through WhatsApp, so WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy also apply.
  • Service providers that help us run Webxeen: our website host (Vercel, servers mainly in the United States), and providers for AI language processing, WhatsApp Business messaging and email (including Cloudflare for email forwarding). They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

  • While a seller uses Webxeen: customer history (past orders and conversations the assistant remembers) is kept for the memory period of the seller’s plan: 3 months on Silver, 1 year on Gold and 5 years on Premium. Older history is deleted.
  • When a seller stops using Webxeen: the seller’s shop information and their customers’ data are deleted within 30 days, and removed from backups within 60 days. Before deletion, the seller can ask us for a copy of their data.
  • Billing records (invoices and payment records) are kept for 5 years, because tax law requires it.
  • Messages sent to us directly (for example demo requests) are kept for up to 12 months unless you become a seller.

7. Security

Each business has its own separate setup — its own number, products, prices and customers. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No online service can be completely secure, so please tell us at once if you think something is wrong.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Customers of a seller: contact the seller first about your orders. You can also email us and we will help the seller respond.
  • Everyone: you can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to legal limits.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK), you may have more rights, such as objecting to some uses or complaining to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email hello@webxeen.com.

9. International processing

Webxeen serves businesses in many countries, and our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

10. Children

Webxeen is a service for businesses and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect information from children for our own purposes.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will change the date at the top, and tell sellers about important changes.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests: hello@webxeen.com. See also our Terms.
